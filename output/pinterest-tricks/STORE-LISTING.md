## Full Description

```
Unlock the full power of Pinterest with 50 tips, tricks, and hidden features — delivered as a free floating widget right on pinterest.com.

WHAT YOU GET
• 50 curated tips covering Pinterest features, browser shortcuts, and productivity tricks
• 5 random tips shown each session — something new every time you browse
• Prev/Next navigation to work through all 5 tips at your own pace
• Works instantly on pinterest.com — no sign-up, no account, no setup

PINTEREST HIDDEN FEATURES
• Search inside a board to find saved pins fast
• Create secret boards for gifts and private projects
• Board sections to organize by subtopic
• Visual search inside any pin image
• Collaborate on boards with friends
• Pinterest Trends tool for discovering what's popular now

BROWSER TRICKS FOR PINTEREST
• Open pins in new tabs with middle-click
• Bookmark your favorite boards with Ctrl+D
• Translate foreign pins with one right-click
• Restore accidentally closed tabs with Ctrl+Shift+T
• Zoom into small thumbnails with Ctrl++

PRODUCTIVITY TIPS
• Use precise keywords for better search results
• Manage notification overload in Settings
• Download your full Pinterest data archive
• Use Chrome tab groups for inspiration sessions
• Reverse image search any pin with one right-click

WHY THIS EXTENSION
Most Pinterest users only use 10% of what the platform offers. This extension surfaces the other 90% — the search tricks, hidden settings, and browser shortcuts that save time and make Pinterest more powerful.

The widget appears as a small floating button in the corner of pinterest.com. Click it to see 5 tips. Click the × to close. If you want to hide it for 48 hours, click the small x next to the button.

Free. No data collected. No account required.
```

## Permission Justifications

**storage**
> Stores user preferences such as dismissed tip state and last viewed tip index. No personal data is collected or transmitted.

**Host permission: `*://*.pinterest.com/*`**
> The extension needs access to pinterest.com pages to inject a floating tips widget that displays tricks and hidden features directly within the Pinterest interface.
