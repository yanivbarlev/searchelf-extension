## Name
Tips for Netflix - Tricks & Hidden Features

## Summary (≤132 chars)

## Description

Discover 50 Netflix tips, tricks, and hidden features — delivered as a sleek floating widget right on netflix.com.

HIDDEN FEATURES & SECRET CODES
Unlock hidden genre categories using secret URL codes, access the debug menu, customize subtitle appearance, manage viewing restrictions, and discover Netflix features most users never find.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
Control playback with keyboard shortcuts — play/pause, skip intro, toggle fullscreen, mute/unmute, adjust volume, and seek forward or back without touching your mouse.

ACCOUNT & PROFILE TRICKS
Learn how to lock your profile with a PIN, delete viewing history, remove titles from Continue Watching, transfer profiles, manage connected devices, and fine-tune your recommendation algorithm.

STREAMING & QUALITY TIPS
Force HD streaming, check your current resolution with the debug overlay, control data usage, download for offline viewing, and adjust playback speed.

BROWSER POWER TIPS
Use Picture-in-Picture mode, pin your Netflix tab, cast to TV, organize with tab groups, zoom into details, and more browser tricks that enhance your streaming experience.

WHY THIS EXTENSION
- 50 curated tips displayed 5 at a time
- Floating widget appears directly on netflix.com
- Works instantly — all tips are built-in, no loading
- Free forever, no sign-up required
- Lightweight: no data collected, no background processes
